Nativity Advent

Paper-pieced foundation quilt — Paper Panache pattern designed by Linda Worland. One block a day for Advent, Dec 1–25, then the blocks join into five large units. This page is a plain-language index of the pattern's Piecing Orders sheet — the join orders and assembly, in summary form.

The finished quilt. Night sky, the Eastern Star blazing top-center with rays down to the stable, two angels, a palm tree, Mary in blue holding Jesus with Joseph beside her, the cradle in front, kings and a camel at right, shepherds and animals at left, the city on the horizon.

How the sheet works

  • One block per Advent day, Dec 1–25. Each block lists its fabric color codes beside the date on the pattern sheet.
  • All diagrams are shown wrong side up — the layouts are mirrored compared to the finished quilt.
  • Heavy line = section seam (cut apart on these lines, sew back together after paper piecing). Thin line = paper piecing seam. Match points where facing arrows (or line and arrow) meet. Dashed line = embroidery line.
  • Final assembly: (Unit 1 + Unit 2) + Unit 3 + (Unit 4 + Unit 5).

Watch-outs the sheet itself flags

  • Dec 11, 2nd Shepherd: when piecing the A section, use a glue stick to stick down the long narrow point before sewing sections together. Also, when joining section C to A-B, there is a very small bit at the top of A (under the arrowhead) that is part of the seam between them.
  • Dec 19, Camel: the Riding King (Dec 14) sits in the dashed space marked on the Camel block, wrong side up.
  • Dec 24/25: the Joseph block carries two Mary sections (Mary G and Mary H), so Mary is not complete until Joseph is pieced.
  • Dec 18, Candle: the breather day — a single section.

The five large units

  • Unit 1: (1Stars A-C + 2Angel D) + [(1Stars D + 2Angel A-C + Palm Tree) + 1Stars E]
  • Unit 2: Riding King + Camel D-F + Camel G-I + Camel J-K, PLUS Camel A-C + Standing King, PLUS Donkey + Kneeling King
  • Unit 3: Eastern Star + Dove + (Joseph + Mary/Jesus) + Cradle + Dog C-D
  • Unit 4: (1Angel D + 2Stars A-C) + (1Angel A-C + 2Stars D-E) + (Your Window + City)
  • Unit 5: (Candle + Cow A-D) + Cow E-F + (Dog A-B + Cat), PLUS 2Shepherd A-C, PLUS Crook + [(2Sheep A-B + 2Shepherd D-E) + 2Sheep C-E], PLUS (1Shepherd E-F + 1Sheep A-B) + (1Shepherd A-D + 1Sheep C-D)

One gap

The pattern's full color-code key is not on the Piecing Orders sheet — each block lists its codes, but decoding every code needs the pattern's color chart page. The codes visible on the cover are: br = brown, br2 = dark brown, brk = brick red, ch = charcoal.
