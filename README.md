# Arts and Crafts

A small collection of beginner sewing projects. Each project shows its finished product first — tap a project card to open its supplies list, step-by-step picture storyboard, and numbered instructions.

## Projects

1. **Mini Travel Jewelry Organizer** — a small fabric pouch with three curved pockets, a snap closure, and a key-ring tab. Green gingham outside with a floral lining.
2. **Structured Zippered Pouch** — a boxy plaid pouch with a cream lining, a center zipper, zipper-end tabs, and a handle.
3. **Green Floral Boxy Pencil Pouch** — a boxy pencil pouch with a metal zipper and boxed corners, shown in a set of four.

The step pictures are illustrations matched to the projects. Where a measurement or material was not available for a project, the page says so rather than guessing.
